## Introduction
The earthquake caused significant damage, with 32 buildings completely destroyed and 11 others suffering moderate to heavy damage. Debris was scattered across several zones, and the road network was disrupted due to water accumulation. Open-space ground also showed signs of flooding. The disaster grades indicate severe structural and non-structural impacts, consistent with the model's estimates. The most critical affected areas include the north-west and north-east regions, where debris is densely concentrated. These zones require urgent attention to ensure safety and prevent further risks. Authorities are advised to prioritize these areas to mitigate further risks and ensure public safety.
