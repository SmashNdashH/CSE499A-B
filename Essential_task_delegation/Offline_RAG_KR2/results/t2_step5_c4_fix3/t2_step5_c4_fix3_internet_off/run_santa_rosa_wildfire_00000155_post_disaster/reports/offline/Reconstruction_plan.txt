## Plan of Action
The plan of action for the reconstruction of the Santa Rosa wildfire includes several key steps to address the extensive damage caused by the disaster. First, we will initiate debris removal in the identified safe areas to clear the path for emergency shelters and facilitate access for recovery teams. This will be supported by local authorities and contractors specializing in hazardous waste management. Simultaneously, we will assess the safety of the hot spot in the south-east to ensure no residual dangers remain. Next, we will establish temporary shelters in the safe zones using tents and shelter kits to accommodate displaced residents, ensuring compliance with Sphere Handbook 2018 [SPHERE-1-p4] standards for housing. Following this, we will focus on repairing and retrofitting the 3 damaged structures to mitigate future wildfire risks, guided by the Sphere Handbook 2018 [SPHERE-5-p1] and [SPHERE-5-p2]. Finally, we will engage the community in rebuilding efforts, incorporating local expertise and resources, and promoting sustainable practices to restore the affected area. This coordinated approach will ensure a resilient and inclusive recovery process.

## Recommendations
To effectively reconstruct the Santa Rosa wildfire-affected area, immediate action is required to clear debris and establish safe zones for emergency shelters. Funding should prioritize the procurement of tents, shelter kits, and tools for displaced residents. Additionally, resources must be allocated for structural repairs and retrofitting of the three damaged buildings to enhance wildfire resilience, aligning with Sphere Handbook 2018 guidance. Community engagement through capacity-building programs and training will ensure long-term recovery success.
