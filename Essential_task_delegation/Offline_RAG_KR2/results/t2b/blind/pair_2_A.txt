# Reconstruction Plan Report

## Executive Summary
The earthquake caused significant damage, with 33% of buildings completely destroyed, 11% moderately damaged, and 56% remaining intact. The most severe impact was observed in the central region, where the highest density of damage occurred, as evidenced by three distinct hotspots: one in the north-west, another in the west, and the third in the north-east. Emergency shelters should be established in safe areas to ensure the well-being of displaced residents, and immediate transportation recovery should prioritize safe areas in the south-east.

## Assessment of Damage
The earthquake caused significant damage, with 33% of buildings completely destroyed, 11% moderately damaged, and 56% remaining intact. The most severe impact was observed in the central region, where the highest density of damage occurred, as evidenced by three distinct hotspots: one in the north-west, another in the west, and the third in the north-east. These hotspots collectively cover 403997, 22107, and 28381 pixels, respectively, indicating the most concentrated areas of destruction. The majority of destroyed structures exhibited total collapse, while damaged buildings displayed moderate to heavy structural and non-structural damage, including partial collapses and extensive wall cracks. Safe areas were identified in the south-eastern portion of the image, with the closest being 412, 311, and 254 pixels from the nearest estimated damage. Based on the disaster grades provided by the Expert team, buildings estimated destroyed were classified as EMS-98 Grade 5, while buildings estimated damaged were classified as EMS-98 Grades 2 to 4. The intact buildings were classified as EMS-98 Grade 1.

## Reconstruction Needs
The reconstruction needs for the affected area involve addressing the significant damage caused by the earthquake. In the central region, where the highest density of destruction is observed, efforts should focus on clearing debris and stabilizing damaged buildings to prevent further collapse. The intact buildings in the south-eastern portion of the image should receive technical assistance to ensure their safety and readiness for potential future events. For the damaged buildings, repairs and retrofits will be necessary to restore structural integrity, aligning with the Sphere Handbook 2018 [SPHERE-5-p1] and [SPHERE-5-p2]. Local expertise and community involvement will be crucial to guide the reconstruction process, ensuring compliance with building codes and standards. The Emergency Service Team's recommendations for safe areas in the south-east will guide the placement of temporary shelters and aid distribution, while the Expert Team's findings on the extent of structural damage will inform the reconstruction priorities.

## Plan of Action
The reconstruction plan begins with debris clearance in the hotspots to ensure safety and facilitate access for emergency response teams. Safe area 1 in the south-east will serve as a staging ground for this task. Simultaneously, intact buildings in the south-east will receive technical assessments to confirm their safety and suitability for continued use, supported by local experts and community input. For damaged buildings, repairs and retrofits will commence in the central region, prioritizing hotspots to stabilize structures and prevent further collapse. Local contractors will be contracted to execute repairs, adhering to building codes and standards outlined in the Sphere Handbook 2018 [SPHERE-5-p1] and [SPHERE-5-p2]. Coordination with local authorities will ensure alignment with regional reconstruction guidelines and oversight of reconstruction progress.

## Recommendations
To ensure effective and efficient reconstruction, we recommend allocating resources for debris clearance in the hotspots to mitigate further risks and facilitate access for emergency response teams. Intact buildings in the south-east should receive technical assessments for safety, supported by local experts and community input. For damaged buildings, repairs and retrofits should focus on the central region, prioritizing hotspots to stabilize structures and prevent further collapse. Engage local contractors to execute repairs, adhering to building codes and standards outlined in the Sphere Handbook 2018, while coordinating with local authorities for oversight and alignment with regional reconstruction guidelines.
