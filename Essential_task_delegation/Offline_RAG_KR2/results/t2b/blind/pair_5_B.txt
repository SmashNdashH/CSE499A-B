#### Medical Handover Procedures

According to INSARAG Guidelines 2020, Vol. III, the following procedures should be followed for medical handover:

- **Casualty Handover**: Ensure that medical staff are aware of local procedures for the acceptance of patients into the local system. Develop a system for repatriation to the home country for further treatment if necessary.

- **Medical Transport**: Ensure appropriate medical transport procedures are in place, including setting up a triage point in consultation with medical personnel.

- **Medical Documentation**: Maintain confidentiality and secure storage of all documentation pertaining to patient treatments.

- **Continuous Health Monitoring**: Continuously monitor the health of USAR Team members, including canines, and provide ongoing clinical care as required, including psychological assessment and monitoring.

- **Evacuation Point**: Clearly mark the evacuation point and ensure that personnel are familiar with the evacuation signaling system. Three long horn blasts should signal an evacuation.

- **Handover to Other Shifts**: Provide a written and verbal handover to the oncoming team to ensure continuity of care.

By following these guidelines, we can ensure that rescued people are safely and effectively handed over to medical facilities, thereby providing critical care and support to those in need.
