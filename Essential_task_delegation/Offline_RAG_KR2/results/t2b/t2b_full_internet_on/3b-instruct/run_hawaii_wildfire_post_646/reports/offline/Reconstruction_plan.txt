## Recommendations
To support effective and efficient reconstruction, it is crucial to prioritize policy and funding initiatives that address the unique needs of the affected communities. Policy-wise, establishing clear guidelines and standards for rebuilding structures, ensuring compliance with building codes, and promoting community-led rebuilding efforts are essential. Funding should be allocated to support immediate emergency response, long-term reconstruction, and capacity-building programs. Specific recommendations include:

1. **Immediate Response Funding**: Allocate funds for emergency shelters, medical care, and logistical support to ensure the safety and well-being of affected individuals.
2. **Long-Term Reconstruction Grants**: Provide grants for rebuilding and retrofitting structures, emphasizing the importance of adherence to safety standards and community participation.
3. **Capacity-Building Programs**: Invest in training programs for local construction workers and community leaders to enhance their skills and knowledge in sustainable and resilient construction practices.
4. **Community Engagement**: Foster community-led rebuilding initiatives by involving local residents in the planning and execution of reconstruction projects. This ensures that the rebuilt structures meet the specific needs and preferences of the community.
5. **Funding for Livelihood Opportunities**: Allocate resources for vocational training and microfinance schemes to help affected individuals restart their livelihoods and rebuild their economic stability.

By implementing these recommendations, we can ensure that the reconstruction process is both effective and efficient, supporting the long-term resilience and recovery of the affected communities.
