# Expert Team Report: Post-Disaster Satellite Photos, Estimated Damage and Disaster Grades

## Overview
The satellite photos of Joplin, Missouri, following the Joplin tornado show a severe level of damage, with 216 out of 238 estimated damage patches classified as Grade 5 destruction under the EMS-98 guidelines. This indicates that nearly all structures were either severely damaged or completely collapsed. Additionally, 22 patches were categorized as Grades 2 to 4, representing moderate to very heavy damage, including cracks in walls, detachment of roof tiles, and failure of non-structural elements. No structures were found to be intact, suggesting that the tornado caused significant structural and non-structural damage across the entire area. Safe zones were identified, but they were extremely limited, highlighting the catastrophic nature of the disaster.
