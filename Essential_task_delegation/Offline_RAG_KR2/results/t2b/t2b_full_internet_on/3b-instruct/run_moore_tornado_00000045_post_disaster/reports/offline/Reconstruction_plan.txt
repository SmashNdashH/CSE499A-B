# Reconstruction Plan Report

## Executive Summary
The Moore tornado of unprecedented severity left a profound impact on the community, with 89 damage patches assessed, 64 of which were classified as Grade 5 (total or near-total collapse) based on the EMS-98 guidelines. The hotspots in the southern and southwestern regions experienced the most severe damage, characterized by total or near-total collapse, while the northern and western parts showed safer conditions, albeit with residual damage. Immediate attention is required to address the safety risks in the hotspots, including clearing debris and stabilizing structures to prevent further collapse. The Emergency Service Team has identified suitable areas for emergency shelters, hospitals, and transportation recovery, prioritizing the restoration of critical infrastructure and support services. The Reconstruction Plan Report aims to guide the government and policymakers in developing a comprehensive strategy for rebuilding and rehabilitating the affected area, focusing on safety, accessibility, and adherence to local building codes and standards.

The hotspots identified in the southern and southwestern regions, particularly Hot Spot 1, Hot Spot 2, and Hot Spot 3, require immediate attention to prevent further structural failures and ensure public safety. The northern and western parts of the photo, while showing safer conditions, still need to be assessed for potential secondary hazards.
