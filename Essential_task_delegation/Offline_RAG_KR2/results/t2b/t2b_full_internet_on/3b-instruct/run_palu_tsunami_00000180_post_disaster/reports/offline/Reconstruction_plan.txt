The Palu tsunami has inflicted significant damage across the affected area, with approximately 52% of the estimated damage patches categorized as 'destroyed' (EMS-98 Grade 5), indicating substantial structural failures and total or near-total collapses. The hotspots, particularly around the north-east and east of the photo, show the highest concentration of damage. In contrast, safe areas are located in the southern and western parts of the photo, offering a clearer indication of relative safety. Immediate response efforts are focused on prioritizing the hotspots for immediate intervention, including the establishment of emergency shelters and medical facilities in suitable locations. The Emergency Service Team has identified three safe areas for emergency shelters and two for medical facilities, while the Reconstruction Plan aims to address the immediate needs of the affected population, focusing on repairing and rebuilding damaged structures, retrofitting vulnerable buildings, and ensuring the safety and well-being of survivors. For displaced people, it may be necessary to repair collective centers or upgrade pre-existing buildings such as schools for mass shelter. According to [SPHERE-3-p1], [SPHERE-3-p2], and [SPHERE-3-p4], the following actions are crucial:

- **Location and Access:** Ensure that the space immediately surrounding the living space supports safe access to fundamental activities such as cooking, toilets, laundry, and healthcare. [SPHERE-3-p1], [SPHERE-3-p2], and [SPHERE-3-p4]

- **Structural Strengthening:** Identify the structural risks and hazards, and learn from, improve, and innovate local building practices and techniques. Facilitate effective knowledge transfer to promote appropriate building practices. [EMS98-2.3-p2] and [EMS98-vi-slightly-damaging]
- **Technical Assistance:** Ensure that people have access to adequate technical assistance. [SPHERE-5-p2] and [SPHERE-5-p1]

To support effective and efficient reconstruction, it is crucial to allocate sufficient resources and implement robust policies. Policy-wise, governments should prioritize long-term housing solutions, ensuring that displaced individuals have access to safe, affordable, and durable shelters.
