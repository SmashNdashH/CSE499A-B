The reconstruction needs for the beriut explosion require immediate action to address the extensive damage to the built environment. In the north-east and south-east regions, where the majority of buildings have been completely destroyed, efforts should focus on clearing debris and stabilizing the hotspots to prevent further risks. For the remaining structures that sustained moderate to very heavy damage, repairs and retrofits are essential to restore functionality and safety. Given the absence of intact buildings, temporary shelters and medical facilities must be established in safe areas identified in the north-west and west of the photo. The recommendations for policy and funding should prioritize immediate support for debris removal and the establishment of emergency shelters and medical facilities in safe zones to address the urgent needs of displaced residents.
