## Plan of Action
The Plan of Action outlines the following steps to address the severe damage caused by the flood:

1. **Debris Removal and Safety Assessment**: Mobilize specialized teams to remove debris from the hotspots in the south-east and south of the image, prioritizing areas within 353 pixels of the nearest estimated damage. Conduct thorough safety assessments to identify hazardous materials and ensure the health and safety of workers. This step aligns with [SPHERE-3-p1] and [SPHERE-3-p2].

2. **Structural Safety Evaluation**: Deploy structural engineers to evaluate the safety of partially collapsed buildings in the identified hotspots. Focus on buildings classified as Grade 5 and 4 based on the EMS-98 guidelines, ensuring compliance with building codes to prevent further collapse. This phase is supported by [SPHERE-5-p1] and [SPHERE-5-p2].

3. **Temporary Housing Solutions**: Establish emergency shelters in safe areas, such as open ground in the north of the image, to accommodate displaced populations. Provide shelter kits and tents to ensure basic living conditions, aligning with [SPHERE-3-p1] and [SPHERE-3-p2]. Coordinate with local authorities to ensure the availability of resources and support.

4. **Medical Support**: Set up temporary medical points in safe areas to address potential injuries and illnesses. Ensure medical staff are equipped to handle the influx of casualties, with plans in place for patient evacuation and treatment. This step is guided by [INSARAG2B-6.3.1].

5. **Road Network Restoration**: Prioritize the reopening of road networks in the safe areas to facilitate emergency response and aid delivery. Clear debris and assess accessibility, ensuring connectivity for displaced populations and relief teams. This action is supported by [SPHERE-1-p4].

6. **Community Engagement and Capacity Building**: Engage affected communities in planning and executing reconstruction efforts. Provide technical assistance to ensure safe and resilient rebuilding, focusing on local materials and sustainable practices. This step aligns with [SPHERE-5-p1] and [SPHERE-5-p2].

7. **Monitoring and Reporting**: Regularly monitor progress and document findings to guide future reconstruction efforts. Collaborate with local authorities to ensure compliance with building codes and standards, fostering community resilience against future disasters. This phase is supported by [SPHERE-5-p1] and [SPHERE-5-p2].

## Recommendations
To ensure effective and efficient reconstruction, it is recommended to allocate resources for debris removal and safety assessments in the hotspots, mobilizing specialized teams to clear debris and assess the safety of partially collapsed buildings. Additionally, temporary housing solutions must be established in safe areas, providing shelter kits and tents to displaced populations. Funding should also support the deployment of structural engineers for safety evaluations and the setup of temporary medical points to address potential injuries. Road network restoration must be prioritized to facilitate emergency response and aid delivery. Community engagement and capacity building programs should be implemented to ensure safe and resilient rebuilding, focusing on local materials and sustainable practices. Regular monitoring and reporting will help track progress and ensure compliance with building codes and standards.
