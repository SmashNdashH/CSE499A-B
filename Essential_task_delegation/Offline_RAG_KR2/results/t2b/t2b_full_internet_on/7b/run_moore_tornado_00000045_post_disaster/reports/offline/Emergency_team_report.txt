# Emergency Service Team Report: Shelters, Hospitals and Transportation Recovery

## Emergency shelters
The emergency shelters should be established in the safe areas identified in the north-west of the photo, specifically around coordinates (100, 165), (195, 282), and (236, 101). These zones are at least 304, 212, and 165 pixels, respectively, from the nearest estimated damage, ensuring safety for displaced residents. According to Sphere Handbook 2018, Shelter and Settlement, standard 3, living space must accommodate essential activities such as sleeping, food preparation, and storage, while maintaining privacy and protection from the elements. The minimum living space should be 3.5 square metres per person, excluding cooking and bathing areas, and the internal floor-to-ceiling height should be at least 2 metres. These guidelines will help ensure that the shelters provide adequate living space for the affected population.

## Hospitals
Based on the disaster assessment and the recommendations from the INSARAG guidelines, it is essential to establish temporary medical facilities in the safe areas identified in the north-west of the photo to treat injured individuals. Given the severity of the destruction in the south and south-west regions, where 550004 and 15069 pixels of the area were classified as Grade 5 destruction, it is critical to prioritize the provision of medical care in these zones to prevent further harm. According to INSARAG [INSARAG3-6.2-p2], it is crucial to check medical evacuation plans, conduct risk assessments, and ensure hygiene standards are maintained at base and worksites. Additionally, medical staff must be aware of local procedures for accepting patients into the local system and develop a system for repatriation to the home country for further treatment. To facilitate the handover of rescued people to hospitals, a triage point should be established in consultation with medical personnel, and appropriate medical transport procedures must be implemented.

## Immediate transportation recovery
The immediate transportation recovery should prioritize reopening the road network in the safe areas identified in the north-west of the photo, particularly around coordinates (100, 165), (195, 282), and (236, 101). These zones are at least 304, 212, and 165 pixels, respectively, from the nearest estimated damage, ensuring safety for emergency responders and displaced residents. Given the severe destruction in the south and south-west regions, where 550004 and 15069 pixels of the area were classified as Grade 5 destruction, the priority should be on facilitating access to these zones to enable the delivery of aid, medical supplies, and other critical resources.
