# Alerts Team Report: Danger Area Alert and Social Media News

## Danger area alert
The most dangerous areas to avoid include the hotspots of severe impact, particularly in the north-west and the west. These zones, classified under EMS-98 Grade 5 and Grades 2 to 4, respectively, indicate total or near-total collapse and moderate to very heavy damage to buildings. Immediate attention is required for rescue operations, debris clearance, and structural safety assessments in these areas to prevent further risks and ensure the well-being of affected residents. Safe areas, such as those in the south-east of the photo, remain distant from the estimated damage, but they should also be monitored for potential secondary impacts.

## Social media news
The Noto earthquake has caused significant damage to several buildings in the affected area. In the north-west and west regions, numerous structures have been classified under EMS-98 Grade 5 and Grades 2 to 4, indicating total or near-total collapse and moderate to very heavy damage. Rescue teams are urgently needed to assess the situation and clear debris safely. Meanwhile, safe areas in the south-east of the photo remain unaffected, but they must be monitored for any potential secondary impacts. Please stay informed and follow official updates for further guidance.
