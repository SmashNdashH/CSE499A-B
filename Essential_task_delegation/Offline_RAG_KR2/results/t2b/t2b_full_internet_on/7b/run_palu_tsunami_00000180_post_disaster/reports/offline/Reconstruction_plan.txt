Strengthen local capacity through skills enhancement programs, focusing on sustainable housing solutions. Utilize remote sensing tools to track debris removal and structural improvements. Establish a feedback mechanism to address concerns and adjust plans as necessary. By following these stages, we aim to rebuild the affected area sustainably and mitigate future risks, leveraging local resources and community participation to achieve long-term resilience.
