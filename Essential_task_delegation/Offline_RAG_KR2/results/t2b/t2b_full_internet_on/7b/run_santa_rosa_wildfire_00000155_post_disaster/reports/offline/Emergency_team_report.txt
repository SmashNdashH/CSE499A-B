# Emergency Service Team Report: Shelters, Hospitals and Transportation Recovery

## Emergency shelters
The emergency shelters should be established in the safe areas identified in the post-disaster assessment. According to the Sphere Handbook 2018, Shelter and Settlement, safe areas are located around pixel coordinates (100, 923), (100, 773), and (250, 923) in the south-west of the photo, with distances of 386, 300, and 251 pixels from the nearest estimated damage, respectively. These areas are crucial for providing temporary housing for displaced residents while ensuring safety and accessibility. The minimum living space requirement is 3.5 square metres per person, excluding cooking space, bathing area, and sanitation facilities, and the internal floor-to-ceiling height should be at least 2 metres. These guidelines will help in creating shelters that are safe, adequate, and culturally acceptable for the affected population.

## Hospitals
Based on the disaster assessment and the guidelines provided by INSARAG, it is essential to establish medical points in the safe areas identified for the immediate handling of casualties. Given the severity of the damage and the need for urgent intervention, priority should be placed on setting up temporary medical facilities in the vicinity of safe area 1, located around pixel coordinates (100, 923) in the south-west of the photo, at a distance of 386 pixels from the nearest estimated damage. These facilities should be equipped to handle the influx of injured individuals and provide initial medical care. Additionally, it is critical to ensure that medical staff are aware of local procedures for accepting patients into the local system and that a system for repatriation to the home country for further treatment is developed. Regular communication with local health authorities and coordination with logistics teams will facilitate the smooth operation of these medical points.

## Immediate transportation recovery
The immediate transportation recovery should prioritize reopening the road network in the safe area 1, located around pixel coordinates (100, 923) in the south-west of the photo, at a distance of 386 pixels from the nearest estimated damage. This area is critical for facilitating the movement of emergency responders, supplies, and displaced residents. Ensuring the accessibility of this road will enable swift delivery of aid and support to the affected population, while also allowing for the safe evacuation of injured individuals to nearby medical facilities. Prioritizing this road will minimize logistical bottlenecks and enhance overall recovery operations.
