## Danger area alert
The earthquake has caused significant damage, with 33% of buildings completely destroyed, 11% moderately damaged, and 56% remaining intact. The most severe impact was observed in the central region, where the highest density of damage occurred, as evidenced by three distinct hotspots: one in the north-west, another in the west, and the third in the north-east of the image. Immediate attention is required for these hotspots due to the risk of further collapse and debris. Safe areas identified in the south-eastern portion of the image, such as those near (917, 923), (821, 807), and (722, 694), are recommended for temporary shelter.
