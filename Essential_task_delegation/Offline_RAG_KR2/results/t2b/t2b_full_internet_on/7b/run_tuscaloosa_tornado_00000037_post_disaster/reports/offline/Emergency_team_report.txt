## Emergency shelters
The emergency shelters should be established in the safe areas identified in the post-disaster assessment, specifically around pixel coordinates (346, 119) in the north, (359, 454) in the centre, and (416, 650) in the centre of the photo. These areas are 146, 131, and 124 pixels, respectively, away from the nearest estimated damage, ensuring minimal disruption to the affected population. According to Sphere Handbook 2018, Shelter and Settlement, the minimum living space should be 3.5 square metres per person for the intact patches, excluding cooking space, bathing area, and sanitation facility. This ensures that each affected household has adequate living space to perform basic domestic activities with dignity. The location and settlement planning guidelines suggest maintaining privacy and dignity of separate households, with each shelter opening onto common space or a screened area, not onto the entrance of another shelter. This will facilitate safe living and minimize risks for all potentially vulnerable groups.
