# Reconstruction Plan Report

## Executive Summary
The tornado caused widespread destruction across the affected area, with 54 of the observed damage patches classified as completely destroyed, indicating total or near-total collapse. Another 50 patches sustained moderate to very heavy damage, including partial collapses and severe structural failures. Only 13 patches remained intact, showing negligible to slight damage. Urgent intervention is required for the hotspots of highest damage concentration, particularly in the east and south-west of the photo, to address the total or near-total collapse of structures and ensure safety. Immediate efforts should focus on establishing emergency shelters, hospitals, and transportation recovery in safe areas to support affected residents.

## Assessment of Damage
The tornado caused widespread destruction across the affected area, with 54 of the observed damage patches classified as completely destroyed, indicating total or near-total collapse consistent with EMS-98 Grade 5 [EMS98-masonry-grade-5]. Additionally, 50 patches sustained moderate to very heavy damage, including partial collapses and severe structural failures, corresponding to Grades 2 to 4 [EMS98-masonry-grade-2] [EMS98-masonry-grade-3] [EMS98-masonry-grade-4]. Only 13 patches remained intact, showing negligible to slight damage [EMS98-masonry-grade-1]. The hot spots of highest damage concentration include zones around (774, 531) in the east of the photo, with 223,555 pixels, and around (41, 750) in the south-west, with 200,020 pixels. Safe areas with minimal proximity to the estimated damage are located around (346, 119) in the north, with 146 pixels, and around (359, 454) and (416, 650) in the centre, with 131 and 124 pixels respectively. These findings align with the recommendations from the Expert team and the guidelines provided in [EMS98-masonry-grade-1], [EMS98-masonry-grade-2], [EMS98-masonry-grade-3], [EMS98-masonry-grade-4], and [EMS98-masonry-grade-5].

## Reconstruction Needs
The reconstruction needs for the affected area include the repair, retrofit, and rebuilding of severely damaged structures in the hotspots of highest destruction, particularly in the east and south-west of the photo. Given the extent of the damage, it is essential to prioritize the establishment of emergency shelters, hospitals, and transportation recovery in safe areas identified in the post-disaster assessment. The repair of partially collapsed structures and the retrofitting of buildings to enhance resilience against future hazards are critical tasks. Local authorities and affected communities should be involved in the planning and execution of these efforts to ensure the reconstruction aligns with building codes and standards. The provision of technical assistance to support the affected population in rebuilding their homes and restoring their livelihoods is also paramount.

## Plan of Action
The Plan of Action outlines the sequential steps for reconstructing the affected area, prioritizing the repair, retrofit, and rebuilding of severely damaged structures while ensuring the safety and well-being of the population. First, we will mobilize resources to assess the structural integrity of the destroyed buildings in the hotspots, particularly in the east and south-west of the photo, using advanced remote sensing technology and expert teams. Based on the findings, we will categorize the repairs and retrofits required for each building, adhering to [EMS98-masonry-grade-5] and [EMS98-masonry-grade-4] standards to ensure stability and safety. Simultaneously, we will initiate debris clearance in the affected zones, focusing on hazardous materials and ensuring compliance with environmental sustainability protocols. Next, we will establish temporary shelters in safe areas to accommodate displaced residents, guided by [SPHERE-1-p4] and [SPHERE-5-p2], and begin reconstructing damaged buildings using locally sourced materials and skilled labor. Finally, we will collaborate with local authorities to develop long-term housing plans and integrate community participation to ensure the reconstruction aligns with building codes and enhances resilience against future disasters.

## Recommendations
To ensure effective and efficient reconstruction, we recommend allocating substantial funding for structural assessments and repairs of severely damaged buildings, prioritizing the hotspots in the east and south-west of the photo. Engage local authorities and affected communities in planning and executing repairs, adhering to building codes and standards. Provide technical assistance to rebuild homes and restore livelihoods, focusing on locally available materials and skilled labor. Additionally, invest in debris clearance and environmental sustainability measures to mitigate further risks. Establish long-term housing plans with community participation to enhance resilience against future disasters.
