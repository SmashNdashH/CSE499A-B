## Recommendations
To support effective and efficient reconstruction, we recommend a multi-faceted approach involving policy reforms, targeted funding, and community engagement. Policy-wise, immediate steps should include revising building codes to incorporate seismic resilience standards, ensuring that all future constructions are built to withstand future earthquakes. Additionally, establishing a dedicated fund for post-disaster reconstruction and rehabilitation, separate from general disaster relief funds, will streamline financial allocations and ensure dedicated resources for rebuilding efforts.

Funding is crucial for both immediate relief and long-term recovery. We propose allocating a significant portion of the national budget towards reconstruction, with a focus on rebuilding critical infrastructure such as schools, hospitals, and public utilities. International aid should also be leveraged, with a particular emphasis on grants and low-interest loans for rebuilding projects. Community participation should be encouraged through microfinance initiatives and skill-building programs to empower local residents in the rebuilding process.

Community engagement is vital for successful reconstruction. Local authorities should collaborate with NGOs and international organizations to organize workshops and training sessions on safe construction practices and disaster preparedness. This will not only enhance the quality of reconstruction but also foster a sense of ownership and responsibility among the community. By adopting these recommendations, we can ensure that the reconstruction process is both effective and sustainable, addressing the immediate needs while laying the groundwork for long-term resilience.
