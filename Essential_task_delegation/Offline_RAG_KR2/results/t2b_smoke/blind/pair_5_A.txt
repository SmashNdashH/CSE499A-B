## Conclusion
The earthquake has caused severe damage, particularly in the central region, where the highest concentration of destruction is evident. Immediate priority should focus on the hotspots in the north-west, west, and north-east, as they represent the most critical areas of structural collapse and debris. Efforts must include clearing debris, assessing safety, and stabilizing damaged buildings to prevent further risks. Additionally, monitoring nearby intact structures for potential hidden vulnerabilities is essential to ensure overall stability and safety.
